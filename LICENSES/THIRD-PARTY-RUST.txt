Rust crates in pyroid's extension module
========================================

pyroid's extension module (pyroid._pyroid) is built from the 49 Rust crates
listed below: the normal dependencies of crates/pyroid-py on the targets pyroid
ships wheels for. They are linked into the module, except the procedural macros
(pyo3-macros and the crates it uses), which generate part of its code at build
time. Where a crate offers a choice of licences, the licence shown is the one
pyroid uses. Crates that ship the same licence text are listed together,
followed by that text.

Generated by cargo-about from Cargo.lock, about.toml and about.hbs. Do not edit
by hand; CONTRIBUTING.md says how to regenerate it.
